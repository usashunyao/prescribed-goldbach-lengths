# Prescribed Goldbach Lengths in the Nonnegative Polynomial Semiring

## Research Paper

This paper studies additive representations of polynomials in the semiring of nonnegative integer polynomials as sums of multiplicative atoms.

We establish a constructive classification of the possible representation lengths for nonconstant polynomials with constant coefficient at least two.
